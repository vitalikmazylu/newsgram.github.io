Account Deletion Request

Application: Newsgram

What happens after deletion?

  • Your profile and personal settings will be permanently removed.
  • All saved news, bookmarks, and preferences will be erased.
  • Accees for your News Ai Aanlize and Ai Editor will be cleared.

How to delete your account?

Method 1: Via the App

Open the app and go to:
Settings -> Account -> Delete Account.
This is the fastest way to process your request.

Method 2: Via Email Support

If you no longer have access to the app, please send a request to our support team:

mvsoft2024@gmail.com

Please include the Email address associated with your account. Request processing time is up to 7 business days.
